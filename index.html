About the Pianist

Dimitris Papakyriazis is an award-winning Greek pianist recognized for his expressive artistry and technical command. He is currently pursuing advanced studies at the prestigious Krzysztof Penderecki Academy of Music in Kraków, having previously graduated with highest honors from the Athens Conservatoire and the London College of Music. Dimitris Papakyriazis has built a vibrant international presence. A frequent first-prize winner in national competitions, he has performed at major venues and festivals across Europe, including the Athens Concert Hall (Megaron), the Greek National Opera Alternative Stage, and the Duettissimo Festival in Poland. Equally at home in solo recitals and chamber music collaborations, Dimitris Papakyriazis continues to captivate audiences with a diverse repertoire spanning classical masterpieces and 20th-century works.
